### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Engineering student** from Turkey and the **solo founder & lead developer** of [**IX Roleplay**](https://ixroleplay.com) — a Turkish GTA V / RageMP roleplay server with **~6,000 registered players**, a persistent economy, and a full custom-scripted gameplay layer I've been building and maintaining for over 1.5 years.

I like systems that are hard to get right: concurrency-heavy game servers, low-level mod pipelines, and anything where a single bad decision cascades into visible pain for real users.

- 🎮 **Shipping:** Custom RageMP framework — drift system, anti-dupe economy, damage system with bitwise bone-encoding workarounds, LOD-optimized vehicle pipeline
- 🛩️ **Building:** Fixed-wing autonomous UAV with **TEKNOFEST Savaşan İHA** team — PX4 · Cube Orange+ · Jetson Orin NX · ROS2
- 🎨 **Modding:** GTA V asset optimization in Blender + Sollumz — polycount reduction, DXT texture compression, collision meshes, shader-level glass behavior
- 📚 **Studying:** Algorithms, AI search (A*, UCS, genetic), ODEs, MIPS assembly — Computer Engineering, 2nd year
- 💭 **Philosophy:** *If it can be modded, it can be optimized. If it can be optimized, ship it to users tonight.*
